# Product Roadmap

## Phase 1: MVP

- **Hex grid tactical map** — Hex-based map with character placement and navigation
- **Initiative and turn order** — Characters act in GURPS initiative order; user controls all characters
- **Movement with maneuver constraints** — XCOM-style movement where clicking to move provides visual feedback on how distance affects available maneuvers (1 step = most maneuvers available, up to half Move = All-Out Attack still possible, full Move = only Move and Attack remaining, etc.)
- **Maneuver system** — Attack, All-Out Attack, Move, Move and Attack, and other core GURPS maneuvers with proper rules enforcement
- **Melee and ranged combat** — Attack resolution with skill rolls, modifiers, damage, and defense rolls; ranged attacks with range modifiers and ammo tracking
- **GCS/GCA character import** — Import character stats, skills, equipment, and attacks from GCS/GCA files with proper data relationships (attacks linked to equipment, awareness of what's in hand, etc.)
- **Resident Evil-inspired scenarios** — Initial scenario content and map design inspired by Resident Evil

## Phase 2: Post-Launch

- **Physics-based ballistics** — Realistic bullet trajectory modeling based on recoil, player skill, and physics; hit location determined by where the bullet actually strikes rather than random rolls
- **Scenario editor** — Tools for building custom maps and encounters
